About Me

I'm a learning designer who can also code — and a technologist who understands how people actually learn. With a PhD in Curriculum & Instruction, an MS in Educational Technology, and a BSc Hons in Computing, I bring a rare combination of research depth and technical fluency to everything I build.

Over 10+ years, I've designed and managed learning programs across K–12, higher education, nonprofits, and community organizations — from teaching computer science in Nepal, to building a 3-year literacy program for 150+ women, to now overseeing CS and STEAM curricula reaching ~4,000 learners annually at Code For Fun. I've led 50+ instructor training workshops, delivered 49,000+ student instruction hours per year, and built programs from scratch in fast-growing environments where nothing existed before.

Why AI + Learning

AI is changing what people need to learn — and how they learn it. But most organizations are stuck between two extremes: ignoring AI entirely, or adopting it without any instructional framework. The gap isn't technology. It's learning design.

My work sits at this intersection. I bring both the learning science and the hands-on technical fluency most AI-adoption efforts are missing — so I can design systems that help people develop real fluency with AI: not just awareness, but the ability to collaborate with it effectively.

I've put this into practice: I built Zero to Live, an open-source interactive course that teaches anyone to build professional websites using Claude Code. I designed the curriculum, wrote the content, and built the entire site — all through human-AI collaboration. It's not just a project about AI. It's a project made with AI, proving the methodology it teaches.

I took the same approach to teaching AI itself with AI Decoded, a free 6-lesson crash course that walks curious professionals from neural networks to LLMs to agents — using the same instructional design that makes K–12 curriculum effective to give adults an accurate mental model of how the technology actually works.

I believe the most important skill in the age of AI isn't syntax — it's strategy. The people and organizations that thrive will be the ones who learn how to learn with AI. That's the problem I want to solve.

AI Literacy

AI Decoded: A Crash Course in AI Fundamentals

Designed and built a free, interactive 6-lesson course that teaches curious professionals how AI actually works — from neural networks to LLMs to agents.

Challenge

Most AI explainers are either hand-wavy hype or dense math that assumes a CS background — leaving curious non-experts without a real mental model of how AI works.

Approach

  • Scaffolded 6 lessons from “what is AI?” to agents
  • Paired plain-language explanations with expandable deep dives
  • Added knowledge checks with retry on every lesson
  • Gated completion so quizzes must be passed to advance

Impact

  • A free, no-prerequisite path to genuine AI fluency
  • Active learning with progress saved in the browser
  • Single HTML file, zero dependencies, deployed for free
